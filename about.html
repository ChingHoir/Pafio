About Me

Hi, I'm Huy Chanchhinghoir, a fourth-year Information and Communication Engineering student at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC), and I'm looking for a Web Developer Internship.

I build full-stack web applications from start to finish: designing the database, writing secure REST APIs with NestJS, Express.js and Spring Boot, and building fast, responsive interfaces with Vue.js, Nuxt and Next.js. Projects like Farm-Link and Local Cam E-Commerce taught me how to ship real features as part of an agile team.

I care about clean, maintainable code and I enjoy turning complex problems into simple, working software. I'm a fast learner who is always picking up new tools, and I'm ready to contribute to a team from day one.

I speak Khmer and English and I'm based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia.
